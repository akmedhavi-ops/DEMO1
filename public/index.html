Our Story

About KAASHI

Kaashi by Laxmi Medhavi is a premium ethnic wear brand rooted in the rich textile traditions of India. We offer a refined selection of sarees and suits, featuring crafts like Banarasi, Chanderi, Maheshwari, Silk and handloom weaves — each piece a tribute to India's diverse heritage.

Our collections blend cultural richness with timeless elegance, crafted in collaboration with skilled artisans from across the country.

At Kaashi, we believe tradition should not only be preserved, but worn proudly. Through every weave, we invite you to wear heritage with grace.
